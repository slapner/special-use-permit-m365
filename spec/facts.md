## Notes and caveats

- **Word Online (Business) is the only Premium piece** in the planned flow set (SharePoint, Outlook, Approvals, Forms, Teams, Adobe/DocuSign are Standard). If a flow that merges a Word template or converts to PDF is added, the flow owner (and the users who run it) need Power Automate Premium. A free alternative is to skip PDF conversion (SharePoint's built-in PDF conversion via Graph isn't covered here: UNVERIFIED).
- **Content controls for the Word template:** use Plain Text, Drop-Down, Combo Box, Image and Repeating Section only. Do not use Date Picker or Check Box; put the date in as plain text from `formatDateTime()`. Give every control a unique Title. Build the template in Word desktop with the Developer tab enabled.
- **Forms and external applicants:** if applicants are outside the org and must attach documents, a Forms upload question will not work. Use an OneDrive or SharePoint file request link, or a Power Apps portal or other route.
- **E-signature choice:** SharePoint eSignature has sequential signing, external guest signers and $2 per request, but no Power Automate hooks and no government-cloud statement. Adobe Sign (GCC only, not GCC High) and DocuSign (GCC, GCC High, DoD) have Standard connectors with signing-order support. Check which tenant type you are in before choosing.
- **Approvals are not signatures.** No Microsoft page claims legal e-signature status for Approvals.
- **Source dates:** the Word Online page metadata shows `updated_at` 2026-11-05; the eSignature pages `updated_at` 2026-09-28; the pay-as-you-go page 2026-10-30; the SharePoint limits page ms.date 2026-10-06. The Forms and LVT support pages show no dates.
- **URLs that returned 404 and were not used:** learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/populate-word-template; learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/manage-lists-with-many-items; learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/manage-large-lists-and-libraries; support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/move-or-copy-files-in-sharepoint.
- **Coverage limits:** the Adobe Sign connector page was read verbatim to about 160K of 184K characters; the Power Automate GCC table was parsed locally from the downloaded HTML. Findings tagged "search snippet" or "community" were not verified against an opened page.
